Website Domains

If you are in a city, you will often see signs with text on them. Unfortunately, if you don't know some of the language, a lot of what you see will be useless to you. However, there are somethings that can be universally seen regardless of the language. One such thing is websites. At the end of websites, you will often see domains such as .com and .org. Some sites use a country specific domain. You can use this information to help identify the country you are in.

Some Domains are Easy

There are some domains that can be easily recognized based on the English spelling of the Country. For example, .uk for the United Kingdom and .fr for France. However, not all domains are this straightforward. Some countries use domains that are not immediately obvious, such as .ch for Switzerland and .de for Germany. It is good to learn the domain names for the most common countries because most websites outside the US will use their country-specific domain rather than .com or .org.

Here is a list of some common country-specific domains:

  • .uk - United Kingdom
  • .fr - France
  • .ch - Switzerland
  • .de - Germany
  • .jp - Japan
  • .au - Australia
  • .at - Austria
  • .ca - Canada
  • .br - Brazil
  • .in - India
  • .cn - China
  • .ru - Russia
  • .za - South Africa
  • .mx - Mexico
  • .es - Spain
  • .it - Italy
  • .se - Sweden
  • .no - Norway
  • .fi - Finland
  • .dk - Denmark
  • .be - Belgium
  • .nl - Netherlands
  • .pt - Portugal
  • .is - Iceland

This is not an exhaustive list, but covers many of the countries you will see.
