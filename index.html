GeoGuessr Guide

Intro

GeoGuessr is a browser game where you are given a street view from Google Maps and you have to guess the location. There are many strategies that can be used to easily locate your position. This guide shows some of those strategies.

Meta and Non-Meta Strategies

All strategies in GeoGuessr can be categorized into two type. These are meta and non-meta strategies. Meta strategies are those that use information specific to the game, such as the the quality of the camera used for the street view and the details about the Google car. Bascially, if you were actually there in real life, you would not be able to use these strategies. Non-meta strategies are those that can be seen in real life. Things such as text on signs, licence plates, and building architecture are examples of non-meta strategies.
